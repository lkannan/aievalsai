When I was working through **retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)**, the hardest
part wasn't any single technique — it was seeing how the pieces fit together.
Naive retrieval, re-ranking, query rewriting, agentic loops: each makes sense on
its own, but the *relationships* between them are what took a diagram to
understand. So I built one.

## What it covers

The atlas lays out the common RAG building blocks — stores, gates, LLM calls,
agentic steps — and how they compose into recognizable patterns. Click a block
to see what it does and where it fits.

## Why this lives in "Labs"

These interactive documents come out of my learning sessions: each one is a
self-contained simulation or map for a concept I was trying to understand. They
live under [Labs](/labs) rather than as ordinary posts, because they're meant to
be *used*, not just read. This post is the written companion — the atlas itself
is the artifact.
